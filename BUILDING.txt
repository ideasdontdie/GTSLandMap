There are two builds of the dll:
  plugin/        Small and Medium sizes: camera, zoom, panning, day and night sheets.
  plugin_tiles/  Large and Full sizes: the same, plus the sharp map tiles read while in view.

1. Put CommonLibSSE-NG 13.1.0, unmodified, in lib/commonlibsse-ng:
     git clone https://github.com/alandtse/CommonLibSSE-NG lib/commonlibsse-ng
     git -C lib/commonlibsse-ng checkout 12d61f3c39f7d4ba1b1ab543df2bca0e613dc747
2. For plugin_tiles only: unpack the official libwebp 1.6.0 Windows package
   (libwebp-1.6.0-windows-x64.zip from the WebM project) into lib/.
3. In plugin/ or plugin_tiles/, run: xmake build
   (xmake and Visual Studio 2022 with C++). The dll lands in build/windows/x64/release/.

Third-party licences (spdlog, DirectXMath, DirectX Tool Kit, libwebp) are in
NOTICE.txt, as shipped with the mod.
